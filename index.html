Shopping guides

A sizing guide for BBDBuyEU finds

Compare BBDBuyEU shoes by insole length in centimeters when the seller provides it. Use a similar shoe that already fits as your reference, and compare EU sizes using the chart for the selected model.

Lay a hoodie or T-shirt flat and measure it from armpit to armpit. Compare that figure with the listing's flat chest width. For jackets, check where the sleeve measurement starts. For jerseys, compare body length, especially if you plan to layer underneath.

How to read BBDBuyEU QC photos

Before following a product link, record the item ID, selected color and size. When warehouse quality-check photos arrive, check the shoe size label and compare the images with the colorway you selected.

Ask for a close-up of stitching or a clasp if the photos are unclear. For headwear, check the stated head circumference and adjustability. For a necklace, verify the chain length and clasp type. Check the seller's return window before approving shipment.

BBDBuyEU shipping to Europe

Use the destination postcode and the dimensions of the packed box when estimating parcel shipping. Ask whether the carrier bills by actual or volumetric weight, especially if shoes are packed with their boxes.

When comparing delivery quotes for Germany, France or the Netherlands, check the fees included on each route. Estimate the total delivered cost from the item price, domestic delivery, any service fees, international shipping and applicable charges omitted from the quote.

Frequently asked questions

What is a BBDBuyEU spreadsheet?

It is a directory of product links, usually grouped by category, that helps shoppers shortlist items before opening the live listings.

How do I use a BBDBuyEU spreadsheet for the first time?

Choose a category, open the current listing, and verify the item ID, color, size and seller terms. Save the selected option so you can compare it with any warehouse photos later.

What should I look for in BBDBuyEU QC photos?

Check that the selected color and size match. Inspect the label, seams and hardware, and ask for a ruler photo if a measurement matters. Photos alone cannot verify authenticity.

How can I estimate BBDBuyEU shipping to Europe?

Enter the actual destination and packed parcel dimensions in the provider's shipping calculator. Compare the billable weight and check which delivery fees, taxes and handling charges the quote includes.

Is this the official BBDBuyEU website?

This is an independent shopping guide. The browsing links open CAFANS pages, and the brand photos illustrate styles for comparison.
